# Brushfall is in early access

Thanks for trying Brushfall this early! Every release published here is an **early-access preview**. It's ready for families to host and play, but it isn't finished, and you should know a few things before you start.

## What "preview" means

- **Things will change.** Quests, classes, balance, menus and settings can change from one version to the next. Some features are still being painted in.
- **You may find bugs.** Please tell us about them: [open an issue](https://github.com/Twelve47Studios/brushfall_release/issues/new/choose), or use **Report a Problem** in the game and forward it from your Studio Desk.
- **Keep backups.** Your server backs up automatically every day, but copy a backup somewhere safe before each update. See [Backups](https://github.com/Twelve47Studios/brushfall_release/wiki/Backups).
- **Your world should carry over.** We aim to keep every world, account, hero and painting working across updates. In a preview we can't promise that a rare change will never need a fresh start, and if one ever does, the release notes will say so clearly before you update.
- **Settings may be renamed.** If a setting in `.env` or `brushfall.config.json` changes, the release notes will say what to do.

## The license is a draft

Brushfall is free for personal, family and non-commercial hosting. The terms are in [LICENSE.txt](LICENSE.txt).

**That license is still a DRAFT. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer yet**, and its wording will change before Brushfall leaves early access. The spirit won't: free for families, your art is yours, your data stays on your computer. A summary in plain words is on the wiki: [License in Plain Words](https://github.com/Twelve47Studios/brushfall_release/wiki/License-in-Plain-Words).

When the final license is ready, it will ship with a release and be called out in the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md).

## What stays the same

- **No ads, nothing for sale in the game.** Donations are welcome, but they never unlock anything.
- **Your server never phones home.** It only asks "what's the newest version?" when it checks for updates. Reports reach us only when a grown-up chooses to send one.
- **Your world lives in one folder on your computer**, and it's yours.
